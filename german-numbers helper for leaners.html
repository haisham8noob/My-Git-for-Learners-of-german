Counting in German, 1 to 1000 by Haisam Rasheedh

German says the small number first, then the big one. 41 is spoken "one-and-forty". Tap any number to see it taken apart and hear it said aloud.

The pattern for 21 to 99

ones+und+tens =einundvierzig(one + and + forty = 41)
  • purple = numbers you simply memorize (1 to 12).
  • 13 to 19 are "ones + ten": dreizehn = three-ten = 13. Same idea as English "thir-teen".
  • In the middle of a number, 1 is ein, not eins: einundzwanzig = 21.
  • Small spelling changes: sechs becomes sechzehn / sechzig, sieben becomes siebzehn / siebzig, and 30 is dreißig (not dreizig).
  • green = hundreds. Say the hundreds first, then the last two digits follow the rules above: 121 = einhunderteinundzwanzig (one-hundred-one-and-twenty). 1000 is eintausend.
  • German numbers are written as one long word, with no spaces. Note that 101 ends in eins (einhunderteins), because the 1 is at the end again.
